Research

Test-Format Anticipation in Visual Working Memory Capacity

Sep. 2026 – Present

Awh/Vogel Lab, University of Chicago · Advisor: Edward K. Vogel, Ph.D. · Supervisor: Gengshi Hu, B.S.

Using EEG and eye-tracking, this study asks whether anticipating the format of an upcoming memory test changes how much people hold in visual working memory. I run participant sessions and maintain the Python (PsychoPy) experiment, including frame-exact display timing.

Effect of Physical Dorm Environments on Out-group Interactions

Jul. 2026 – Present

Environmental Neuroscience Lab, University of Chicago & Duke University · Advisors: Marc G. Berman, Ph.D. & Sarah E. Gaither, Ph.D. · Supervisor: Elizabeth A. Janey, B.S.

This multi-site study examines how the physical design of residence halls shapes cross-race and cross-class interaction among college students. We are also testing whether adding greenery to shared living spaces changes how students from different socioeconomic backgrounds perceive one another.
