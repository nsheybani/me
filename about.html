About me

Hi! My name is Navid [nuh-veed] and I am a First Year at the University of Chicago — graduating in Spring 2030. My research interests lie in how attention, memory, and decision-making get measured — through neuroimaging, real-world behavioral methods, and on-site clinical surveying — and in who gets left out when those measurements shape public health and disability research.

If you were to ask me my interests before 9 am and after 5 pm, I would probably tell you writing satire and updating my Japanese stationery collection. I am currently seeking Summer 2027 research positions.
